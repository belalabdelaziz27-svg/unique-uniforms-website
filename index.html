Tailoring services

Three services at our shop, with a price list you can check before you come in.

See tailoring pricesSend us a photo (opens in a new tab)
  • Alterations Clothes taken in, let out, shortened or reshaped.
  • Repairs Zips, seams, tears and fastenings fixed.
  • Custom tailoring Clothes made to your measurements.

One suit or fifty, it comes down to the same careful work.

  1. We measure

    Good fit starts with a tape measure and a conversation about how the garment will be worn.

  2. We cut and sew

    Patterns, seams and finishing get the same attention whether it's a repair or a new uniform.

  3. We check the fit

    We look at the garment on the person wearing it and adjust what needs adjusting.
