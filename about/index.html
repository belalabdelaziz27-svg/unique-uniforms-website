About Unique Uniforms & Tailoring

Corporate uniform and fitting solutions in Dubai

A Dubai business providing professional uniform solutions, custom tailoring, alterations and garment support, from our shop in Al Barsha South (Arjan), near Dubai Science Park.

Who we work with

We support businesses across healthcare and medical laboratories, aesthetics and wellness, hospitality and corporate sectors, along with retail, schools and industrial teams.

We take on both small and large requirements. Fittings, tailoring and alterations happen at our own shop, and larger orders may involve our manufacturing partners.

Our aim is simple: to help your business keep a consistent, professional appearance, with uniform support that stays flexible and practical as your team changes.

Individuals are welcome too. Alterations, repairs and custom tailoring are available in-store, under the name Unique Tailoring Dubai, and the garments on our work page were made here.

What we provide

Uniform orders are quoted individually, usually from about 25 pieces or sets, with smaller requirements considered case by case.
