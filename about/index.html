A neighbourhood tailor that also dresses whole teams

Unique Uniforms & Tailoring is a shop in Al Barsha South, Dubai. Individuals come to us for alterations, repairs and custom tailoring, and businesses come to us for uniforms.

Two kinds of customer, one way of working

A shirt being taken in and a uniform for a full team may seem like very different jobs. To us, they start the same way: listening to what the garment has to do, measuring carefully, and paying attention to the details people notice when they wear it.

We look after men, women and children, and we'd rather build a lasting relationship with a customer than simply finish one job. We're a real shop you can walk into. If something can't be done, or isn't worth doing, we'd rather tell you.

How we like to work

  • Attention to detail

    Seams, hems and finishing are what separate clothes that look fine from clothes that look right.

  • Plain advice

    We'll explain what's possible, what it will cost and what we'd do if it were our own garment.

  • Easy to reach

    Call, WhatsApp or walk in. You shouldn't have to chase anyone to find out about your order.
