What to expect

Bring your fabric and a picture, a favourite garment you'd like copied, or just an idea. We'll talk through the style and fabric, take your measurements and explain how fittings will work.

We can help with

  • Clothes made to your measurements
  • Pieces for men, women and children
  • Copying a garment you already love
  • Outfits for weddings and occasions
