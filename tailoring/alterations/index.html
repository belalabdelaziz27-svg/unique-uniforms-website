Tailoring service

Alterations

Clothes taken in, let out, shortened or reshaped.

See our pricesAsk on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab)

What to expect

Bring in the piece that doesn't quite work: trousers that are too long, a dress loose at the waist, a jacket tight across the shoulders. We'll look at it with you, pin it where needed and tell you what can be done.

We can help with

  • Shortening trousers, skirts and sleeves
  • Taking in or letting out waists and seams
  • Reshaping shirts, jackets and dresses
  • Adjusting straps and necklines
  • Resizing formal and occasion wear
Owner to confirm this list

Before you come in

  • Bring the shoes you'll wear with the outfit if the length matters.
  • Have an event coming up? Tell us the date when you get in touch.
  • A photo by WhatsApp is an easy way to ask whether something can be altered.

What it costs
