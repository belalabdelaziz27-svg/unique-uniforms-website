Three easy steps

  1. Ask, then bring it in

    Send a photo by WhatsApp or call us if you have a question, then bring the garment to the shop.

  2. We advise and confirm the price

    We'll tell you what can be done, what it costs and when it will be ready.

  3. Collect from the shop

    Come back for your garment and try it on. If the fit isn't right, tell us.

Personal tailoring and alterations are in-store only. Bring your garment to our shop and collect it from there. We don't offer pickup or delivery for alterations. Fabric is generally not included in our listed prices. We are not VAT registered.
