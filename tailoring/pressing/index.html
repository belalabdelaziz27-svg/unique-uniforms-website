Pressing

What to expect

Drop in the clothes you need looking their best, from everyday shirts to a suit for an important day. We'll steam press them and tell you when they'll be ready to collect.

We can help with

  • Shirts and blouses
  • Suits and jackets
  • Trousers and skirts
  • Dresses and formal wear
  • Items needing extra care: just ask
Owner to confirm this list

Before you come in

  • Point out any delicate fabrics or embellishments when you drop off.
  • Need something by a certain time? Ask when you drop it off.
  • Combine pressing with an alteration or repair on the same visit.

What will it cost?

It depends on the garment and the work involved. Ask us and we'll give you a price for your item.
