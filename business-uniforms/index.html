What to expect on a bulk order

  • About 25 pieces or sets

    Our approximate minimum per uniform order, and the minimum for embroidery or logo printing. Smaller orders are considered case by case, and we can't accept every one.

  • Fabric

    For larger corporate orders, we can supply or arrange the fabric. On small orders, you normally provide the fabric.

  • About 1 to 6 weeks

    Estimated production time, depending on quantity and specifications. We'll confirm the timing for your order, and it remains an estimate.

  • Collection or local delivery

    Collect from our shop, or have your bulk order delivered within the UAE. Delivery isn't free: its cost is accounted for in your quotation.

The questions that make a uniform work

  • Fabric

    How often the garment is washed, the heat of the workplace and how long it has to last. On larger orders we can supply or arrange the fabric; on small orders, you provide the fabric.

  • Fit

    Your team won't all be one shape. We can talk through measuring staff, sizing ranges and adjustments for individuals.

  • Branding

    Your logo, colours and where they sit on the garment, plus staff names if you want them. Send what you have and tell us how you'd like it to appear.

  • Repeat orders

    Teams change. If you expect to add staff later, tell us now so we can plan with reordering in mind.

Branding and customisation

Uniforms that fit your brand, your garment specifications and the way your team works. Tell us how you'd like your brand to appear, and we'll confirm the options for your garment and fabric in your quotation.

  • Embroidery, on bulk orders of 25 pieces or sets or more
  • Logo printing, on bulk orders of 25 pieces or sets or more
  • Colour matching to your brand
  • Custom garment cuts
  • Staff names on uniforms

Uniform support that continues

Fittings, alterations and replacements from the same shop that quoted your order.

  • Staff fittings

    Measuring and fitting for your team at our shop, so uniforms fit the people wearing them.

  • Alterations and repairs

    Adjustments for individual staff and repairs to keep uniforms in service.

  • Replacement uniforms

    New starters and worn-out pieces, matched to your existing order.

  • Express tailoring support

    Urgent alterations when express service is available. Ask us when you need it.

From first message to quotation

Simple enough to forward to whoever signs off the budget.

  1. Send your brief

    Tell us your industry, the garments you need, a rough quantity and when you need them. A rough idea is enough to start.

  2. Talk it through

    We come back with questions about fabric, fit and branding, and what we'd suggest for your team.

  3. Receive your quotation

    You get a written quotation based on what we've agreed, including what's possible for your timeframe.

  4. Approve and proceed

    Once you're happy with the details, we confirm next steps with you, including how staff fittings will work.

Common questions

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Uniform orders start at about 25 pieces or sets, and so do embroidery and logo printing. Smaller orders are considered case by case, and you'd normally provide the fabric. Alterations and personal tailoring are separate, with their own price list.

Which industries do you supply?

Hotels, hospitality, restaurants and catering; healthcare, hospitals and medical laboratories; aesthetics, wellness, salons and spas; retail and cosmetics; corporate and office teams; schools; industrial and professional workwear; security and drivers. If your team isn't listed, ask us anyway.

Do you supply the fabric?

For larger corporate orders, yes: we can supply or arrange the fabric, and it's included in your quotation. On small orders, you normally provide the fabric.

How long does an order take?

Production usually takes about 1 to 6 weeks, depending on quantity and specifications. We'll confirm the timing for your order, and it remains an estimate.

Can you add our logo?

Yes. Embroidery and logo printing are available on bulk orders of 25 pieces or sets or more. Send us your logo and tell us where you'd like it, and we'll confirm the options for your garment and fabric.

Can you match our brand colours, change the cut or add staff names?

Yes. We can match colours to your brand, adjust the cut of a garment to suit your team and the work they do, and add staff names to uniforms. Tell us what you need when you request a quotation.

Do you deliver?

Local UAE delivery is available for bulk uniform orders. It isn't free: the cost is accounted for in your quotation. You can also collect from our shop in Al Barsha South. Personal tailoring and alterations are in-store only.

Can we see samples first?

Ask us when you send your request, and we'll let you know what we can show you for the garments you're considering.

How much do uniforms cost?

Every uniform order, including single uniform sets, is quoted individually. The price depends on garment type and design, materials and fabric, quantity, customisation, embroidery or printing, and other specifications.

Do you charge VAT?

We are not VAT registered.

Tell us about your team.

A rough idea is enough. We'll come back with questions and a quotation.
