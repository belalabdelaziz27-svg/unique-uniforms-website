The questions that make a uniform work

  • Fabric

    How often the garment is washed, the heat of the workplace and how long it has to last. We'll go through suitable options with you.

  • Fit

    Your team won't all be one shape. We can talk through measuring staff, sizing ranges and adjustments for individuals.

  • Branding

    Your logo, colours and where they sit on the garment. Send what you have and tell us how you'd like it to appear.

  • Repeat orders

    Teams change. If you expect to add staff later, tell us now so we can plan with reordering in mind.

Branding and customisation

Tell us how you'd like your brand to appear. We'll confirm what's possible for your garment and fabric when we quote.

  • Embroidery, on orders of 25+ pieces
  • Logo printing, on orders of 25+ pieces

Also ask us about

  • Colour matching to your brand
  • Custom cuts and sizing adjustments
  • Name details for individual staff

From first message to quotation

Simple enough to forward to whoever signs off the budget.

  1. Send your brief

    Tell us your industry, the garments you need, a rough quantity and when you need them. A rough idea is enough to start.

  2. Talk it through

    We come back with questions about fabric, fit and branding, and what we'd suggest for your team.

  3. Receive your quotation

    You get a written quotation based on what we've agreed, including what's possible for your timeframe.

  4. Approve and proceed

    Once you're happy with the details, we confirm next steps with you, including how staff fittings will work.

Common questions

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Bulk uniform orders start at about 25 pieces. That's an approximate guide rather than a fixed rule for every job, so ask if you need fewer. Alterations and personal tailoring are available separately, with their own price list.

How long does an order take?

Production usually takes about 1 to 6 weeks, depending on quantity, specifications and what the order requires. We'll confirm the timing for your order in your quotation.

Can you add our logo?

Yes. Embroidery and logo printing are available on bulk orders of 25 pieces or more. Send us your logo and tell us where you'd like it.

Do you deliver?

You can collect from our shop in Al Barsha South, or we can deliver to local locations. Tell us where the uniforms need to go, and we'll confirm coverage and any fees.

Can we see samples first?

Ask us when you send your request, and we'll let you know what we can show you for the garments you're considering.

How much do uniforms cost?

It depends on the fabric, design, branding and order volume, so we quote each order rather than publish fixed bulk prices.

Tell us about your team.

A rough idea is enough. We'll come back with questions and a quotation.
