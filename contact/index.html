Directions

We're in Shop 10, The Light Commercial Tower, in Al Barsha South Third (Arjan), near Dubai Science Park. The tower is behind Mediclinic Parkview Hospital. Look for the Unique Uniforms & Tailoring name on our glass door.

For the most reliable route, open directions in Google Maps (opens in a new tab). If you get lost, call us on +971 4 582 0555.

Personal tailoring and alterations are in-store only. Bring your garment to our shop and collect it from there. We don't offer pickup or delivery for alterations. Local UAE delivery is available for bulk uniform orders only.
