What to expect on a bulk order

  • About 25 pieces or sets

    Our approximate minimum per uniform order, and the minimum for embroidery or logo printing. Smaller orders are considered case by case, and we can't accept every one.

  • Fabric

    For larger corporate orders, we can supply or arrange the fabric. On small orders, you normally provide the fabric.

  • About 1 to 6 weeks

    Estimated production time, depending on quantity and specifications. We'll confirm the timing for your order, and it remains an estimate.

  • Collection or local delivery

    Collect from our shop, or have your bulk order delivered within the UAE. Delivery isn't free: its cost is accounted for in your quotation.
