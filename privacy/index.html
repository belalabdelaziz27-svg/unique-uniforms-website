Privacy notice

How we handle the details you give us.

Draft for review.

This text is a draft and hasn't been reviewed by a legal adviser. It must be reviewed against UAE data protection requirements before the site launches.

Who we are

Unique Uniforms & Tailoring, Shop 10, The Light Commercial Tower, Al Barsha South Third, Dubai, UAE. Contact: info@uniqueuniformsme.com.

What we collect

The details you choose to give us when you contact us, such as your name, company, phone number, email address and the details of your request.

How we use it

We use your details to answer your request, prepare a quotation and provide the service you asked for. We won't add you to marketing lists unless you've separately agreed to it.

Contacting us through other services

If you contact us by WhatsApp, email or phone, or use Google Maps to find us, your message or request also passes through that provider and its own privacy terms apply.

Website services

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to fetch them. Our hosting provider may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses. It doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers or marketing cookies at this time. We will update this notice if that changes.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as we need them to deal with your request and meet our legal and business record-keeping obligations. Retention periods to be confirmed.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it or delete it, by emailing info@uniqueuniformsme.com.
